of submission eligibility. Author affiliation, authorship, funding, historical

months of meaningful public development cannot be replaced by this draft.

validation or coverage-calibrated interval is asserted. The dataset example

## Living-manuscript maintenance

The manuscript is a working scientific narrative, not an automatically
regenerated ledger. After each **completed and reviewed** scientific assessment:

1. Record raw/source provenance, protocol, all positive and negative outcomes,
   restrictions and uncertainties in `validation/`.
2. Update `docs/JOSS_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md` with what the result does and does
   not substantiate.
3. Change `paper/paper.md` only if the evidence materially supports, qualifies
   or contradicts a manuscript claim; cite the primary publication and keep
   detailed per-spectrum tables in the validation archive.
4. Have the author check scientific interpretation and keep unresolved human,
   licensing and submission requirements visible. An open or unmerged
   assessment PR is not automatically adopted as a manuscript result.

The extended discussion and comparison table in `paper.md` are **working-draft
material**. Before JOSS submission, compress the table and discussion to the
journal's required software-paper scope. Do not remove adverse evidence to
save words.

## Possible Surface Science article

A longer methods/software article for *Surface Science* could emphasize
surface-kinetic assumptions, reproducible examples, model identifiability and
experimentally grounded comparison. This is a **potential separate venue and
manuscript strategy**, not a claim that the current JOSS-formatted manuscript
meets that journal's scope or author requirements. Before choosing a venue,
verify the journal's current article types and policies, then establish a
specific contribution and sufficiently strong independent scientific review.
Do not submit substantially overlapping manuscripts to two journals
concurrently. Reuse the evidence archive while preparing a distinct
venue-appropriate structure.
