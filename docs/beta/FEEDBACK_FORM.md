## Optional scientific or data feedback

- Is there a model assumption, fitted value, unit or uncertainty claim that
  you think should be reviewed? A comparison to an independent calculation is
  particularly useful.
- If you have shareable spectra, please **ask before attaching them** so we can
  agree on permission, attribution, storage and any publication use. Private
  debugging does not confer permission to publish or redistribute your data.

## Sending comments

Use **Report a Bug / Send Feedback** in the app to inspect and copy a diagnostic
report, or send a short email to **karol.j.cieslik@gmail.com**. You can paste
answers from this form or simply send a few sentences. Nothing is submitted
automatically, and no spectra are required. Review screenshots, typed text and
exports for confidential information before sharing.
