# Narayanan et al. (2025): pure methyl-mercaptan ice

Paper: [Thermal Desorption Kinetics, Binding Energies, and Entrapment of Methyl Mercaptan Ices](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adc7b6).
Actual extracted version: [arXiv:2504.01102v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01102v1),
draft 3 April 2025, PDF page 5, Fig. 2. S. Narayanan, E. L. Piacentino,
K. I. Öberg and M. Rajappan; [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
as explicitly identified by the arXiv HTML. Do not describe this as digitization
of the publisher's final-layout PDF.

The light-pink experimental curve is identified by the legend 11 ± 2.1 ML.
Temperature is K; signal is in units of 10^15 molecules/K, as printed. The
methods specify 2 K/min (0.0333333 K/s). No time channel was constructed.

[Data](digitized.csv) retain 908 recovered columns and 16 blank columns.
[QA overlay](extraction_check.png), [quality](quality.json) and
[provenance](provenance.json) preserve source placement and colour-threshold
sensitivity. Engineering axis placement allowance is about 0.16 K plus pixel
half-width 0.054 K. Signal extraction bounds are in the CSV and exclude the
experiment's own uncertainties. In particular, steep/overlapped parts of the
falling edge have missing coordinates and must not be silently interpolated.

This supplies a potential zero-order depletion-shape case, not a completed
energy validation. The figure is an experimental coverage series; one selected
curve is extracted here. No kinetic fit was run. Source PNG is retained unchanged;
coordinate extraction and annotations are derived changes.
